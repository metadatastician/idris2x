= Best-practice comparison
:toc: macro

How idris2x measures against named industry frameworks, as of 2026-11-16. *Present* means the file or mechanism exists in
this repository today. *Planned* means designed in an ADR but not built. *Gap* means neither. Nothing here is a
certification: badges and scores are earned by external tools, not by this table.

toc::[]

== Repository and community health (GitHub community standards, CHAOSS basics)

[cols="3,1,4",options="header"]
|===
| Practice | Status | Where

| Licence detectable by the forge; REUSE-style per-file SPDX | Present | `LICENSE`, `LICENSES/`, SPDX headers

| CODEOWNERS, labels, triage automation | Present | `.github/CODEOWNERS`, `.github/labels-idris2x.toml`, estate label set
| Governance and maintainer handoff | Present | `docs/MAINTAINERS.adoc` (abandonment contract), `.github/GOVERNANCE.adoc`

== Toolchain-distribution practice (compared with rustup, uv, Zig, ghcup, mise)

[cols="3,1,4",options="header"]
|===
| Practice | idris2x | Best-in-class reference
| Per-artifact digest pinned in a project lockfile | Planned (prototyped) | uv, Bazel `http_archive`, Nix fixed-output derivations
| Signed metadata, *enforced* by the client | Planned (prototyped, drilled) | ghcup (GPG, strict mode); rustup signs but does not enforce
| Rollback and freeze protection | Planned (prototyped, drilled) | TUF; most toolchain managers have neither
| Mirrors untrusted by construction, ordered fallback | Planned (prototyped, drilled) | Zig community mirrors, Bazel
| Offline / air-gap path as a first-class command | Planned (prototyped, drilled) | rare; usually ad hoc
| Typed, stable error IDs with runbooks and exact fixes | Planned (taxonomy and runbooks present) | Rust compiler error index, Elm compiler messages
| Complete, checkable uninstall | Planned (documented) | uv, bun (single directory)
| Chaos drills of failure modes in CI | Runs against both launchers (128 checks; the CI job is added and has not yet run) | uncommon in this category
| Zero telemetry, documented network surface | Designed | uv (opt-in), Zig
|===

== What would move the most gaps

. Apply the rulesets and enable Discussions and immutable releases (owner settings, minutes).
. Register at bestpractices.dev.
. Ship the Rust launcher with the chaos suite as its CI gate (Phase 1).
. Recruit a second maintainer with their own signing key.
